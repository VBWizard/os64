YONDER / CANVAS / POINTER INPUT

A Personal Touch

The drawings have learned to listen. Hold, drag, scribble, and let go.

Enable Scripts and Modern Web, then reload.

1. Leave your mark

Ready for your signature.

Hold the left button and draw. Take the pointer outside the canvas, then return before releasing: the stroke stays yours.

2. A travelling light

Drag anywhere in the night sky.

The light follows your drag and settles when you release. Try leaving the drawing while holding the button.

3. A chord of your own

Left · middle · right

Hold different mouse buttons together. Each held button lights its own circle. Cover the window during a drag to try cancellation.

4. Meet me halfway

Rest your pointer in the lane.

Start the gate, then hold the pointer still in its path. The gate turns green when it passes underneath—even without a mouse move.

Try 25%–400% zoom.
