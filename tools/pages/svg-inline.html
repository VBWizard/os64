SVG: first pixels

These are live inline SVG paths drawn by Yonder. Each caption says what to look for. Try scrolling, resizing the window and changing the page zoom. With Modern Web switched off, the SVG artwork disappears.

1. A little landscape

Blue mountains, an amber sun and a pale winding path. The artwork keeps its proportions.

2. Opacity belongs to the group

Two overlapping blue squares. Their overlap must be the same shade as the rest.

3. CSS wins

A blue bar, then an orange bar with a clear gap. The stylesheet replaces the red fill and the attribute transform.

4. A hole, not white paint

The green frame has a hole showing the amber background. It stays amber on a dark page.

5. Round caps stretch too

A long blue capsule with wide elliptical ends. Ordinary strokes are expanded before scaling.

6. Color follows the document

A blue check mark. With scripts enabled, this button changes its inherited color.
