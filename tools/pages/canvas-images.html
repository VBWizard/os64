Picture This

Borrow a picture. Move its pixels. Make a picture of your own.

Enable Scripts and Modern Web, then reload.

1. A stamp of approval

An SVG becomes an image source. Each click places another rotated stamp; the sixth starts a fresh sheet.

2. Close enough to count

A crop from a tiny canvas, enlarged two ways. The left stays crisp; the right blends neighboring pixels.

3. Copy, scoot, repeat

This canvas copies overlapping parts of itself. The source is snapshotted, so the colors travel without smearing.

4. Out and back again

A drawing is exported as a PNG data URL, decoded as an image, and drawn back here.

Waiting for the first picture.

Try 25%–400% zoom. The magnifier intentionally shows bitmap pixels. Buttons draw immediately; scheduled animation comes later.
