Intl capture

What this browser's Intl answers for en-US, captured as a file: the strings libjs is held to byte for byte (INTL.md § 8). It formats fixed matrices of DateTimeFormat cases (option bags, zones, instants, ranges, parts), of NumberFormat and PluralRules cases (option bags, values, ranges, parts, plural categories), of RelativeTimeFormat and ListFormat cases, of Collator's comparisons and of Segmenter's segments at every granularity, asks the questions other browsers answer differently (an alias zone's name, words in the scripts browsers find with dictionaries), and lists the browser's Intl surface.

Open it from tools/intl_capture_server.py, which serves this directory and saves what the page sends to the file it names; or press Save for a download.
