A Little Shade

A light to chase, a hidden caster, and shadows with holes in them.

Enable Scripts and Modern Web, then reload.

1. Follow the light

The light travels around a raised tile. Its soft shadow moves the other way. Start the light for hands-free motion.

2. Out of sight

An amber ring sits beyond the left edge, yet its blue shadow reaches the window. The button brings the ring inside.

3. A hole lot of shade

These stamps come from another canvas. Transparent holes cast no shadow. Change the stamp and both copies follow.

4. Words with depth

Filled and outlined letters cast colored shadows. The gaps between their strokes stay open.

Try 25%–400% zoom. The light can keep moving while you explore.
