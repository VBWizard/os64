SVG: taking shape

Rectangles, circles, ellipses, lines and polygons, drawn live by Yonder. Try page zoom, scrolling and dark pages. The captions explain what each picture should do.

1. A home for our pixels

A house, amber sun and white cloud. Painted colors stay fixed when the surrounding page changes theme.

2. Corners have limits

Rounded blue, oval orange, shallow green corners and square purple corners. A zero radius on either axis makes square corners.

3. Percentages find their place

A centered blue ellipse and amber circle. Their proportions remain stable as you zoom.

4. Open and closed

Both triangles are filled. The left outline stays open at the bottom; the right outline closes.

5. Lines keep their stroke

Flat blue ends, round orange ends and wide elliptical green ends. The green line is stretched horizontally.

6. CSS changes the shape

The stylesheet makes this blue rectangle narrower than its attributes request. Enable scripts and press the button to toggle its size and color.
