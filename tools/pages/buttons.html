Buttons the page draws

STYLED_BUTTONS.md: a <button> that holds elements, or whose background, border or corners the page styled, is laid out like any element, so its icons, words and colours are the page's. A button of plain text that the page left alone is still the toolkit's button. Every button still takes the click, the focus and the keys. Another browser shows every check PASS, and so should yonder.
