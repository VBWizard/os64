YONDER / CANVAS / THE FRAME CLOCK

In Motion

No rapid clicking required. Four drawings, sharing the browser's frame clock.

Enable Scripts and Modern Web, then reload.

1. The evening tide

Three waves travel together. Their movement follows elapsed time, so a slower frame takes a bigger step.

2. A little orbit

A golden satellite circles a mint planet. Gradients and transformed paths redraw with each frame.

3. Words on the move

HELLO CANVAS sways and turns. The same text renderer that drew the still gallery now draws its next frame.

4. A travelling companion

This one is an HTML element. Its motion uses the same timestamp as the canvas drawings above.

Try pause/resume, reverse, and 25%–400% zoom. Cover the window and uncover it: motion resumes without replaying missed frames.
